# Returns and Refunds

We want you to love your NovaTech product. If you don't, you can return it under the conditions below.

## Return Window

- You can return any NovaTech product within 30 days of the delivery date
- For gifts, the 30 days start from the delivery date shown on the gift receipt
- Products bought during the festive sale (1 October – 15 November) have an extended return window of 45 days

## Return Conditions

- The product must be in like-new condition with no physical damage
- All accessories, the charger, manuals and the original packaging must be included
- The device must be unpaired and removed from your NovaFit or NovaHome account
- Opened products are accepted; a restocking fee of 10% applies only if items or packaging are missing
- Products damaged by misuse, and items marked "final sale", cannot be returned

## How to Start a Return

1. Go to Orders in the NovaTech app or website and choose "Return item".
2. Pick a reason and choose a free pickup slot (India) or print a prepaid label (international).
3. Pack the product in its original box. Pickup usually happens within 2 business days.
4. We inspect the product within 3 business days after it reaches our warehouse.

## Refunds

- Refunds go back to the original payment method
- UPI and cards: 5–7 business days after inspection
- Net banking and wallets: 3–5 business days after inspection
- Cash on delivery orders are refunded by bank transfer within 7 business days
- Original shipping charges are refunded only if the product arrived defective or the wrong item was sent

## Exchanges

- Defective products can be exchanged for the same model within the 30-day window at no cost
- To switch to a different colour or model, return the original item and place a new order
