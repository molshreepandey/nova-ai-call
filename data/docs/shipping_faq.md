# Shipping FAQ

## Where do you ship?

NovaTech ships to India, the United States, the United Kingdom, the United Arab Emirates and Singapore. We do not ship to other countries yet. PO boxes and APO/FPO addresses are not supported.

## Shipping Options in India

- Standard delivery: 3–5 business days, free on orders above ₹999, otherwise ₹79
- Express delivery: 1–2 business days in metro cities (Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Pune), ₹149
- Same-day delivery: available in Mumbai and Bengaluru for orders placed before 12:00 noon, ₹249
- Cash on delivery is available for orders up to ₹20,000

## International Shipping

- United States and United Kingdom: 7–10 business days, flat $15
- UAE and Singapore: 5–8 business days, flat $12
- Free international shipping on orders above $200
- Import duties and taxes are included in the price for the US, UK and Singapore; for the UAE they are collected on delivery

## Order Processing and Tracking

- Orders placed before 2:00 PM IST on a business day ship the same day
- A tracking link is sent by SMS and email as soon as the order ships
- Business days are Monday to Saturday, excluding national holidays

## Delays and Lost Packages

- If your order has not arrived 5 business days after the latest estimated date, contact support
- Lost packages are replaced or refunded in full after the courier confirms the loss, usually within 7 business days
