# NovaBand X200 — Product Specifications

The NovaBand X200 is NovaTech's flagship smart fitness band, designed for all-day health tracking, workouts and sleep analysis.

## Launch and Pricing

- Launch date: 15 January 2025 (India, US, UK, UAE, Singapore)
- Price (India): ₹8,999 including GST
- Price (US): $129
- Colours: Midnight Black, Glacier White, Coral Red
- Model number: NB-X200

## Battery and Charging

- Battery capacity: 300 mAh lithium-polymer
- Typical battery life: up to 14 days with standard use (heart-rate every 10 minutes, notifications on, always-on display off)
- Heavy use: about 7 days with always-on display enabled
- Continuous GPS: up to 20 hours
- Charging: magnetic 2-pin USB-C cradle, 0–100% in about 75 minutes; 10 minutes of charging gives roughly 1 day of use
- Low-battery warning at 10%; power-saving mode extends the last 10% to about 2 days

## Water Resistance

- Rated 5 ATM (water resistant to 50 metres) under ISO 22810:2010
- Suitable for swimming in pools and the sea, showering and rain
- Not suitable for scuba diving, high-speed water sports, hot tubs or saunas
- The X200 is water resistant, not fully waterproof: water damage caused by use beyond the 5 ATM rating is not covered by warranty
- Rinse with fresh water after swimming in salt water or chlorinated pools

## Sensors

- Optical heart-rate sensor (PPG), 24/7 monitoring
- Blood oxygen (SpO2) sensor
- Skin temperature sensor
- 3-axis accelerometer and 3-axis gyroscope
- Built-in dual-band GPS (L1 + L5)
- Ambient light sensor for auto-brightness

## Display and Build

- 1.62-inch AMOLED touchscreen, 192 x 490 pixels, up to 1,000 nits
- Gorilla Glass 3 cover, aluminium frame
- Weight: 26 g (without strap)
- Strap: 22 mm quick-release silicone, fits wrists 130–210 mm

## Connectivity and Compatibility

- Bluetooth 5.3 Low Energy
- Works with the NovaFit app on Android 9.0+ and iOS 15+
- Syncs with Google Health Connect and Apple Health
- Supports 120+ workout modes, sleep staging and stress tracking
