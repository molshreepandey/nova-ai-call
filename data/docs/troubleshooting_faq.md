# Troubleshooting FAQ

## X200 Won't Pair with My Phone

1. Make sure Bluetooth and location permission are turned on for the NovaFit app.
2. Keep the band within 1 metre of the phone and charged above 20%.
3. If the band was paired before, remove "NovaBand X200" from your phone's Bluetooth settings.
4. In NovaFit, tap Devices > Add Device and pick X200. Confirm the 6-digit code shown on the band.
5. Still not pairing? Restart the phone and the band (hold the side button for 8 seconds, then choose Restart).

## X200 Not Charging

- Clean the two charging pins on the back of the band and the cradle with a dry cloth; sweat residue is the most common cause
- Make sure the magnetic cradle snaps into place; a charging icon should appear within 10 seconds
- Use a 5 V / 1 A USB adapter; some laptop ports and fast chargers cut power to small devices
- If the battery was fully drained, leave it on the charger for 15 minutes before it powers on
- If none of this works, the cradle may be faulty. Chargers have a 6-month warranty

## How to Reset the X200

- Soft restart: hold the side button for 8 seconds and tap Restart. No data is lost.
- Factory reset from the band: Settings > System > Reset > Confirm. This erases all data on the band.
- Factory reset without the screen: place the band on the charger and hold the side button for 15 seconds until the logo flashes twice.
- After a factory reset, remove the band from the NovaFit app before pairing again.

## Inaccurate Heart-Rate or SpO2 Readings

- Wear the band a finger's width above the wrist bone, snug but comfortable
- Clean the sensor window on the back
- Tattoos, very cold skin and heavy wrist movement can affect readings
- SpO2 readings are for wellness only and are not a medical device measurement

## NovaHub Setup and Reset

- During setup, the LED ring pulses blue; connect to the hub from the NovaHome app within 10 minutes
- If the LED ring is solid red, the hub has no internet; check the Ethernet cable or Wi-Fi password
- Soft reset: unplug the hub for 10 seconds and plug it back in
- Factory reset: press and hold the recessed reset button on the base for 12 seconds until the ring turns amber. Paired devices must be added again.
