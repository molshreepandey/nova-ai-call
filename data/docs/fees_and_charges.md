# Fees and Charges

## Mutual Funds

- No entry load on any NovaWealth fund
- Direct plans bought through the NovaWealth app have no transaction fee or commission
- The expense ratio is deducted daily from the fund's NAV, not charged separately
- Exit loads apply only if units are redeemed within the exit-load period of that fund
- Stamp duty of 0.005% applies to every purchase, as required by law

## NovaWealth Advisory Plus

- Fee-based advisory with a dedicated advisor and a personalised financial plan
- Minimum portfolio: ₹10 lakh
- Fee: 1% per year of the advised portfolio value, billed quarterly
- Includes 4 review meetings per year and rebalancing recommendations

## Portfolio Management Services (PMS)

- Minimum investment: ₹50 lakh, as required by regulations
- Strategies: NovaWealth Core Equity PMS and NovaWealth Emerging Leaders PMS
- Fee option 1 (fixed): 2% per year of portfolio value
- Fee option 2 (hybrid): 1% per year plus 10% of profits above a 10% annual hurdle rate
- Exit charges: 2% if withdrawn in year 1, 1% in year 2, nil after 2 years

## Other Charges

- No account opening fee and no annual maintenance charge
- No charge for SIP registration, pause, step-up or cancellation
- Physical account statements by post: ₹50 per statement (email statements are free)
