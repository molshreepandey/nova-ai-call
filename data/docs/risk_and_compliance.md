# Risk, Suitability and Investor Protection

## Risk Profiling

- Every investor completes a 10-question risk profile when opening an account
- Profiles: Conservative, Moderate and Aggressive
- Conservative investors are generally suited to liquid and debt funds; Moderate to a mix of debt and large cap equity; Aggressive to a higher equity allocation including flexi cap funds
- The profile can be retaken any time in the app, and should be updated after major life changes

## Important Disclaimers

- Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks; read all scheme-related documents carefully
- Past performance may or may not be sustained in the future and is not a guarantee of returns
- NovaWealth never guarantees or assures returns on any fund or PMS strategy
- Equity funds can fall in value, especially over short periods; they are best suited to goals at least 5 years away
- Projections from calculators are illustrative and assume a constant rate of return, which real markets do not deliver

## Advice Policy

- The voice assistant and phone support provide general information only, not personalised investment advice
- Personalised recommendations are given only by NovaWealth advisors after a risk profile and a financial review
- NovaWealth staff will never ask for your password, OTP or PIN, and will never ask you to transfer money to a personal account

## Grievance Redressal

1. Raise a complaint in the app under Help, or email grievance@novawealth.example.
2. Complaints are acknowledged within 1 business day and resolved within 21 days.
3. If unresolved, escalate to the NovaWealth Investor Relations Officer at iro@novawealth.example.
4. Investors can also approach the market regulator's SCORES portal if not satisfied with the resolution.
