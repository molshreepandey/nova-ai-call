# NovaTech Warranty Policy

This policy applies to NovaTech products bought from NovaTech's official store or an authorised retailer. The warranty period starts on the purchase date shown on your invoice.

## Standard Warranty Periods

- NovaBand X200: 12 months from the date of purchase
- NovaHub: 24 months from the date of purchase
- Chargers, cables and straps: 6 months from the date of purchase
- Refurbished products: 6 months from the date of purchase

To check whether a product is still covered, compare the number of days since the purchase date with the warranty period. For the X200, 12 months is treated as 365 days.

## NovaCare+ Extended Warranty

- NovaCare+ extends the NovaBand X200 warranty to 24 months in total (12 extra months)
- Price: ₹1,499 (US $19) for the X200
- Must be bought within 30 days of the original product purchase
- Includes one accidental-damage repair (for example a cracked screen) with a ₹999 service fee
- NovaCare+ is linked to the device serial number and transfers to a new owner if the device is sold

## What the Warranty Covers

- Manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship
- Battery that holds less than 80% of its original capacity within the warranty period
- Display faults such as dead pixels, flickering or touch not responding
- Sensors that stop working or give clearly faulty readings
- Charging faults not caused by a damaged or third-party charger

## What the Warranty Does Not Cover

- Physical or accidental damage (drops, cracks, crushed devices), unless you have NovaCare+
- Water damage from use beyond the 5 ATM rating, such as scuba diving, saunas or hot tubs
- Normal wear and tear, including scratches, strap wear and cosmetic fading
- Damage from unauthorised repairs, opening the device or modified firmware
- Loss or theft
- Products without a valid proof of purchase

## How to Make a Warranty Claim

1. Open the NovaFit or NovaHome app, go to Support > Warranty Claim, or email warranty@novatech.example.
2. Provide the device serial number, your invoice and a short description of the problem.
3. Our support team responds within 2 business days with a prepaid shipping label.
4. Repairs or replacements are completed within 7–10 business days after we receive the device.
5. Replacement devices are covered for the rest of the original warranty period, or 90 days, whichever is longer.
