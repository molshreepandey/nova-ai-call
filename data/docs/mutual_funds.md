# NovaWealth Mutual Funds

NovaWealth offers five mutual fund schemes. Each scheme has a Direct plan (bought directly from NovaWealth, lower expense ratio) and a Regular plan (bought through a distributor). Returns below are past performance of the Direct plan as of 30 September 2026 and are not a guarantee of future returns.

## NovaWealth Bluechip Equity Fund

- Category: large cap equity, invests mainly in the top 100 listed companies
- Riskometer: Very High
- Benchmark: Nifty 100 TRI
- Launched: March 2014
- Minimum investment: ₹1,000 lump sum, ₹500 per month SIP
- Expense ratio: 0.65% (Direct), 1.85% (Regular)
- Exit load: 1% if redeemed within 365 days of each purchase, nil after that
- Past returns (Direct, annualised): 1 year 11.2%, 3 years 14.1%, 5 years 13.8%

## NovaWealth Flexi Cap Fund

- Category: flexi cap equity, can invest across large, mid and small companies
- Riskometer: Very High
- Benchmark: Nifty 500 TRI
- Launched: August 2016
- Minimum investment: ₹1,000 lump sum, ₹500 per month SIP
- Expense ratio: 0.72% (Direct), 1.95% (Regular)
- Exit load: 1% if redeemed within 365 days of each purchase, nil after that
- Past returns (Direct, annualised): 1 year 13.5%, 3 years 16.0%, 5 years 15.2%

## NovaWealth ELSS Tax Saver Fund

- Category: equity linked savings scheme (ELSS)
- Riskometer: Very High
- Lock-in: 3 years from the date of each investment; every SIP instalment has its own 3-year lock-in
- Tax benefit: investments qualify for deduction under Section 80C up to ₹1.5 lakh per financial year, available only under the old tax regime
- Minimum investment: ₹500 lump sum, ₹500 per month SIP
- Expense ratio: 0.68% (Direct), 1.90% (Regular)
- Exit load: nil (units cannot be redeemed during the lock-in)
- Past returns (Direct, annualised): 1 year 12.4%, 3 years 15.3%, 5 years 14.6%

## NovaWealth Short Duration Debt Fund

- Category: short duration debt, invests in bonds with 1 to 3 years Macaulay duration
- Riskometer: Moderate
- Minimum investment: ₹1,000 lump sum, ₹500 per month SIP
- Expense ratio: 0.40% (Direct), 0.95% (Regular)
- Exit load: 0.25% if redeemed within 30 days, nil after that
- Past returns (Direct, annualised): 1 year 7.6%, 3 years 6.9%

## NovaWealth Liquid Fund

- Category: liquid fund, invests in money-market instruments up to 91 days maturity
- Riskometer: Low to Moderate
- Suitable for parking emergency money or short-term surplus
- Minimum investment: ₹500 lump sum
- Expense ratio: 0.18% (Direct), 0.30% (Regular)
- Exit load: small graded exit load for redemptions within 6 days of investment, nil from day 7
- Instant redemption: up to ₹50,000 or 90% of the holding, whichever is lower, credited within 30 minutes, 24x7
- Past returns (Direct, annualised): 1 year 6.9%

## Direct vs Regular Plans

- Both plans hold exactly the same portfolio and are managed by the same fund manager
- Direct plans have a lower expense ratio because no distributor commission is paid, so over the long term they usually deliver slightly higher returns
- Investors can switch from Regular to Direct; a switch is treated as a redemption, so exit load and capital gains tax may apply
