# SIPs, Redemptions and Transactions

## Starting a SIP

- A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) invests a fixed amount at regular intervals
- Minimum SIP: ₹500 per month for all NovaWealth equity and debt funds
- Frequencies: monthly (any date from 1 to 28), quarterly, or weekly (every Monday)
- SIPs are paid by auto-debit through a bank mandate (UPI AutoPay or NACH); a new mandate takes up to 5 business days to register
- The first instalment can be paid immediately, and later instalments follow the chosen date

## Step-up, Pause and Stop

- Step-up SIP: automatically increase the SIP amount every year by a fixed percentage (5% to 25%) or a fixed amount
- Pause: a SIP can be paused for 1 to 6 months, once every 12 months, without cancelling it
- Stop: a SIP can be cancelled any time in the app; requests must be made at least 7 business days before the next SIP date
- If 3 consecutive SIP instalments fail because of insufficient balance, the SIP is cancelled automatically
- Stopping a SIP does not redeem existing units; they stay invested

## Redemptions (Withdrawals)

- Redeem any time in the app, by units or by amount, subject to exit load and lock-in rules
- Cut-off time: requests received before 3:00 PM IST on a business day get that day's NAV; later requests get the next business day's NAV
- Liquid Fund cut-off: 1:30 PM IST
- Equity funds: money is credited within 3 business days (T+3)
- Debt funds: money is credited within 1 business day (T+1)
- Liquid Fund: credited the next business day (T+1), or within 30 minutes using instant redemption
- Money is always paid only to the investor's registered bank account

## Switches and Systematic Transfers

- Switch: move money between NovaWealth funds; treated as a redemption plus a purchase
- STP (Systematic Transfer Plan): transfer a fixed amount from the Liquid or Debt fund into an equity fund every week or month, minimum ₹500 per transfer
- SWP (Systematic Withdrawal Plan): withdraw a fixed amount every month, minimum ₹1,000 per withdrawal

## NAV and Statements

- NAV (net asset value) is the per-unit price of a fund, published every business day by 11:00 PM IST
- A consolidated account statement is emailed by the 10th of every month in which there was a transaction
- Capital gains statements can be downloaded from the app for any financial year
