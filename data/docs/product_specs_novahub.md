# NovaHub — Product Specifications

NovaHub is NovaTech's smart home hub. It connects lights, plugs, sensors, locks and cameras from many brands and lets you control them from one app or by voice.

## Launch and Pricing

- Launch date: 1 September 2025
- Price (India): ₹12,499 including GST
- Price (US): $149
- Colour: Graphite Grey
- Model number: NH-100

## Connectivity and Protocols

- Wi-Fi 6 (802.11ax), dual-band 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz
- Ethernet: 1 x Gigabit RJ45 port
- Zigbee 3.0 and Thread border router built in
- Matter 1.3 certified controller
- Bluetooth 5.3 for setup and BLE sensors
- Supports up to 128 connected devices (up to 64 Zigbee devices)

## Hardware

- Quad-core 1.8 GHz processor, 2 GB RAM, 8 GB storage
- Dimensions: 110 x 110 x 32 mm, weight 280 g
- Power: 12 V / 1.5 A adapter included
- Built-in 3,000 mAh backup battery keeps automations running for about 4 hours during a power cut
- Status LED ring and a recessed reset button on the base

## Software and Features

- Local automations keep working without an internet connection
- Works with Google Home, Amazon Alexa and Apple Home (via Matter)
- Controlled through the NovaHome app on Android 9.0+ and iOS 15+
- Automatic over-the-air firmware updates
- Privacy: video and sensor data are processed locally by default; cloud backup is opt-in
