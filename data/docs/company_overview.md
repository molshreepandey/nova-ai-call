# NovaWealth: Company Overview

NovaWealth Asset Management is a fictional investment company created for this demo. It offers mutual funds, portfolio management services (PMS) and investment advisory to investors in India and to eligible NRIs.

## About NovaWealth

- Founded in 2012, headquartered at Bandra Kurla Complex, Mumbai
- Assets under management: ₹18,400 crore (as of 30 September 2026)
- Over 2.1 lakh investors
- Branch offices: Mumbai, Bengaluru, New Delhi, Pune and Hyderabad
- Services: mutual funds (direct and regular plans), portfolio management services, and fee-based investment advisory

## Contact and Support Hours

- Investor helpline: 1800-000-6789 (toll free), Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM IST
- Email: care@novawealth.example (replies within 1 business day)
- NovaWealth app and website: invest, track, start or stop SIPs and redeem 24x7
- Walk-in branches are open Monday to Friday, 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM IST
- The voice assistant can answer general questions; for personal advice, investors can book a free 30-minute session with a NovaWealth advisor in the app

## What the Assistant Can and Cannot Do

- Can explain NovaWealth products, fees, account opening, SIPs, redemptions, risk profiles and policies
- Can explain general investing terms such as SIP, NAV, expense ratio, exit load and lock-in
- Can run illustrative calculations, for example how a SIP might grow at an assumed return
- Cannot recommend specific buy, sell or hold decisions for an individual investor
- Cannot predict market movements or guarantee returns
- Cannot access or change a specific investor's account or transactions
