# Account Opening and KYC

## Who Can Invest

- Resident Indian individuals aged 18 or above
- Minors, through a parent or legal guardian; the account converts to the minor's own name at 18
- Non-resident Indians (NRIs), except residents of the United States and Canada, who cannot invest with NovaWealth
- Hindu Undivided Families (HUFs), companies and trusts through the corporate desk

## KYC Documents

- PAN card (mandatory)
- Aadhaar for address proof; passport, voter ID or driving licence are also accepted
- A cancelled cheque or a bank statement showing the investor's name, account number and IFSC
- A recent photograph and signature (captured in the app)
- NRIs additionally need their passport, overseas address proof and an NRE or NRO bank account

## How to Open an Account

1. Download the NovaWealth app or visit the website and sign up with your mobile number.
2. Enter your PAN; if you are already KYC-verified with a KRA, your details are fetched automatically.
3. Otherwise complete video KYC, which takes about 10 minutes between 9:00 AM and 9:00 PM IST.
4. Add a nominee or explicitly opt out of nomination; one of the two is mandatory.
5. Your account is usually activated within 2 business days after KYC approval.

## Nomination and Account Changes

- Up to 3 nominees can be added, with percentage shares that total 100%
- Bank account, address, email and mobile number can be changed in the app; bank changes take up to 10 business days for security checks
- Opening an account is free; there is no annual maintenance charge
